# Rook Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Rook saves videos you share to it from TikTok, YouTube and Instagram. It summarizes them and reminds you about them later. This page explains what data Rook uses and why.

## What we collect

- **Account info:** your email address, plus your name if you sign in with Apple or Google.
- **Your saves:** the links you share to Rook, and the notes, albums, reminders and Ask questions you create.
- **Basic technical logs:** errors and usage counts we need to keep the service running and its costs under control.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos or browsing history. We do not show ads, sell your data or track you across other apps or websites.

## How we use it

- To sign you in and keep your saves synced across devices.
- To create summaries, key points and recipes for each save.
- To answer your Ask questions about your own saves.
- To send the reminders you choose.

## Services that process your data

- **Supabase:** hosting, database and sign-in.
- **Anthropic (Claude):** AI summaries and Ask answers.
- **Google (Gemini and the YouTube Data API):** understanding YouTube videos and fetching video details.
- **Supadata:** fetching what is said in TikTok and Instagram videos so they can be summarized.

These services only receive what they need to do their job. Rook does not store copies of the videos you save. Videos play through each platform's own player, and that platform's privacy policy applies while you watch.

## Your saves are private

Other Rook users cannot see your saves.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Account, then Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything you saved.

## Children

Rook is not intended for children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
